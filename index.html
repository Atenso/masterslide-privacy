Privacy Policy

Masterslide: Art Puzzle Game · AtensoStudio · Version 3.2 · Last updated 26 November 2026

Türkçe · Delete your data

Masterslide: Art Puzzle Game (as listed on Google Play; in Turkish "Masterslide: Zeka Bulmacası") is a sliding puzzle game for Android, developed by AtensoStudio in Türkiye. This policy explains what the game stores, what goes over the internet, why, for how long, and how you can remove it.

The short version. Masterslide can be played without creating an account. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game connects to it automatically (you can also connect in Settings) and keeps a copy of your progress in the cloud (Google Firebase), linked to an account ID created for you. You can delete this copy and the account at any time in Settings → Delete cloud save. The game downloads painting images from our file host, shows ads from Google AdMob, sells optional in-game items through Google Play, and records usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) that are not linked to your name or account, so we can improve the game. We do not sell your data.

1. Without Play Games: what stays on your device

If you are not connected to Google Play Games, your saved game is stored only on your device and is not sent to us (usage statistics are described in section 4, purchases in section 5):

  • Your level progress, stars, best results and statistics
  • Coins, hearts, hints and other in-game items
  • The paintings in your collection
  • The player name and avatar you choose on the profile screen
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, text size) and whether you finished the tutorial

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes all of this. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in the device backup kept in your Google account and restore it when you reinstall; you control this in your device's backup settings.

2. Google Play Games and cloud save

Connecting to Google Play Games keeps your progress in the cloud, puts you on the leaderboards and unlocks achievements. If you are already signed in to Play Games on your device, the game connects automatically when it starts; otherwise you can connect in the game's settings. The game is fully playable without it. When you are connected:

  • Play Games gives the game your Play Games player ID and display name. Your scores and achievements are kept by Google Play Games under its own terms.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) creates an account ID linked to your Play Games identity, so that only you can read your cloud save. To protect sign-in, Firebase also processes your IP address.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google, stored in the European Union) keeps a copy of your game progress: levels, stars, best results, statistics, collection, coins, hearts and other items, the player name and avatar you chose, and the order numbers of purchases already added to your game. Your settings and the level you are currently playing stay on the device.

This copy is used only to restore your progress and keep it in sync between your devices. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone. Google processes it as our service provider; see Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

To delete it, open Settings → Delete cloud save in the game: your cloud save and your Firebase account are removed at once, the progress on your device stays, and cloud saving stays off until you connect again. You can also email us (see the data deletion page); we delete it within 30 days.

3. Painting downloads

Each level reveals a painting. The first 40 are inside the app; the others are downloaded when you get near them, from atenso.github.io, a website hosted by GitHub. The request asks only for the image file and contains nothing about your progress. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information (the browser engine's user agent) when the file is requested; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

All paintings are in the public domain or released under CC0. Their sources (mostly Wikimedia Commons and the Cleveland Museum of Art) are recorded in the game's catalog.

4. Advertising and usage statistics

Masterslide shows ads from Google AdMob, mainly rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example, to continue a level or double your coins), and an ad between levels every few levels. Buying "No Ads" turns off the ads between levels. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. For personalised ads Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Usage statistics. Versions of the game released from November 2026 use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played: which levels are too hard, where players stop, and which features are used. It records game events such as which screens you open, starting, finishing or leaving a level, mistakes and lost hearts, use of hints, rewarded ads offered and watched, opening the store, completed purchases (item, price and currency), the daily reward, the tutorial steps, opening a painting or the settings, tapping the share button and the game language. The Analytics SDK also collects an app-instance ID, your device's advertising ID (only where ad storage is allowed), device model, Android version, app version, and an approximate location (country and city) derived from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored. This data is pseudonymous: we never send your player name, your Play Games or Firebase ID, your email address or any other account identifier to Analytics. Google processes it as our service provider; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent at first launch; personalised ads and usage statistics stay off until you agree. You can change or withdraw your consent for ads and usage statistics at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. Elsewhere ads may be personalised and usage statistics are on; you can limit ad personalisation by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

5. In-game purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Google tells the app which item was bought, its price and currency, and an order number. The app uses these to add the item to your game, keeps the order number in your save (and in your cloud save if you are connected) so that a purchase is never added twice, and records the purchase (item, price and currency) in the usage statistics described in section 4. We never see your name, email address or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Other Google Play features

The game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and it may show Google Play's own review prompt. Google handles these under its own privacy policy; the game does not receive your review.

7. Who else can see your data

Only the services named above: GitHub for painting downloads, and Google for ads, purchases, Play Games, Google Play features and Firebase (cloud save and usage statistics). Google AdMob receives the advertising data described in section 4 and uses it as an independent controller. Apart from that we do not share, sell or rent your data. Data is encrypted in transit. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so.

8. Where your data is processed

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. Cloud saves are stored in the European Union. For transfers from the EEA and the UK, Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. For players in Türkiye, this means that your data is processed by Google (and, for painting downloads, GitHub) outside Türkiye.

9. How long we keep data

  • Cloud save and Firebase account: until you delete them (Settings → Delete cloud save, or by email).
  • Sign-in security data: Firebase may keep sign-in IP addresses for a short time to protect accounts.
  • Usage statistics: kept at user level for at most 14 months; reports that cannot identify anyone may be kept longer.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than 12 months after it is closed.
  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

10. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Cloud save, purchases and Play Games features: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Usage statistics and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Non-personalised ads, security and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the free game and keeping it safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations, where they apply (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to atensostudio@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Players in Türkiye (KVKK)

For players in Türkiye, the data controller under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) is AtensoStudio, Türkiye (atensostudio@gmail.com). Your data is collected automatically through the app for the purposes in this policy, on the grounds of establishing and performing our contract with you (Art. 5(2)(c)) and our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure, funding it with ads and improving it (Art. 5(2)(f)). It is processed by the service providers in section 7, outside Türkiye (section 8). You can exercise your rights under Article 11 by writing to us; we answer free of charge within 30 days, and you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu). The Turkish version of this policy gives the full text.

13. Children

Masterslide is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In the EEA, if you are under the age at which you can consent to online services in your country (between 13 and 16), please ask a parent before accepting the consent message. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

14. Deleting your data

On your device: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Masterslide → Storage → Clear data.

In the cloud (if you connected Play Games): in the game, open Settings → Delete cloud save (immediate), or email us as described on the data deletion page; we delete your cloud save and Firebase account within 30 days. For data held by Google (ads, purchases, your Play Games profile) or GitHub, use their privacy tools linked above.

Usage statistics: they are tied to an app-instance ID, not to your name or account. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling it creates a new ID, and older event data expires on its own within 14 months.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page, its version and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

16. Contact

Data controller: AtensoStudio, Türkiye. Questions or requests: atensostudio@gmail.com

14. Verilerinizi silmek

Cihazınızda: uygulamayı kaldırın ya da Android Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Masterslide → Depolama → Verileri temizle yolunu izleyin.

Bulutta (Play Games'e bağlandıysanız): oyunda Ayarlar → Bulut kaydını sil yolunu izleyin (hemen) ya da veri silme sayfasında anlatıldığı gibi bize e-posta gönderin; bulut kaydınızı ve Firebase hesabınızı 30 gün içinde sileriz. Google (reklam, satın alma, Play Games profiliniz) ya da GitHub'daki veriler için yukarıdaki bağlantılardaki gizlilik araçlarını kullanın.

Kullanım istatistikleri: adınıza ya da hesabınıza değil, bir uygulama örneği kimliğine bağlıdır. Uygulamanın verilerini temizlemek ya da uygulamayı yeniden kurmak yeni bir kimlik oluşturur; eski olay verileri en geç 14 ay içinde kendiliğinden silinir.
